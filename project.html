Walloping Welterweight V2

First Person Boxing Game with a unique camera system, active hitboxes, enemy attack patterns, an intro cutscene, and stylised environment using free assets.

Role: Gameplay Programmer · Unreal · C++ · May 2026
Watch gameplay Résumé

Highlights

The core gameplay mechanics: the dodge mechanic with linked camera movement, two different types of punches the player can use, and the 3 types of punches the AI can use. The AI has a randomized pattern so the player cannot predict what punch the AI is throwing next. Version 2 added better game feel which included a hitstun effect for the AI, new main menu UI, a intro cutscene, boxing gloves that did not make it to build 1, and a impact frame for when the enemy dies.

Most 3D Models are NOT my own. I did model the bench and a chair, but most models were obtained for free through the Epic Games Store. Any assets that are not mine are credited within the project.
